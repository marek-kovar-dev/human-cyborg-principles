# Human Cyborg Principles

This repository hosts foundational documents exploring the technical, ethical, and geopolitical dimensions of human cybernization — the integration of advanced neural interfaces and computational systems with the human body and mind.

## Overview

The materials examine the shift of cybernetic technologies from fragmented, high-cost experimental systems toward scalable, human-centric architectures. Central themes include the protection of human agency, the risks of algorithmic control over biological systems, and the establishment of clear boundaries between human cognition and external computation.

## Contents

- `Declaration_of_Human_Cybernization.pdf`  
  Core principles addressing neural sovereignty, the "Physical Ripcord," the "Born-Human Principle," and constitutional firewalls against the erosion of individual agency.

## Purpose

This work seeks to articulate a coherent framework for the responsible development and deployment of cybernetic technologies, with particular attention to geopolitical realities and the long-term implications for human autonomy.
